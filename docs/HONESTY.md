# Honesty

Precedent: TheoryMine (Edinburgh, theorymine.co.uk, 2010 onward) sold naming rights to new, automatically generated and machine-proved theorems with a PDF certificate for about GBP 15. What differs here: Lean 4 + Mathlib checking, an open registry anyone can re-verify with the standalone verifier, signed attestations, CC0 lemma texts.

- "New": the whitespace-normalised statement is absent from Mathlib sources and from this registry. Nothing more.
- Lemmas are small and instances of known facts. We claim a checked proof and a name, not significance.
- Lean 4 + Mathlib decide validity. Certificates record Lean version, Mathlib commit, file and log hashes.
- The registry is ours; the attestation proves we signed it, not that anyone else recognises the name.
